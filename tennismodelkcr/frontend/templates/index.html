Overperformance Index (OI)

OIt = OIt-1 + K × (result − p̂implied)

Where result ∈ {0,1} is the match outcome and p̂implied is the implied probability derived from closing betting odds. K is a decay constant. OI accumulates across a player's career and measures how consistently they beat market expectations — independent of ranking.

Surface Elo System

Four independent Elo ratings are maintained per player: Hard, Clay, Grass, and Indoor Hard. Ratings are computed exclusively on the 2019–2023 training window to prevent data leakage into the 2025–2026 test set. Expected score uses the standard logistic formula E = 1 / (1 + 10^(−ΔElo/400)).
